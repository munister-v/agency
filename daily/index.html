Lead sheet · 01 Sep 2026

Twelve businesses
worth a first call.

One state and one region, both on Eastern Time: the widest overlap a Kyiv working day has with the United States. Every entry below was checked against the live site on the day of writing, not taken from a directory.

Internal working page. Not indexed, not in the navigation, not in the sitemap. It names real companies, their phone numbers and the weak points of their websites, and none of that belongs in public view.

How this was checked. Every site was fetched on 1–2 September 2026 and read directly: platform from the markup, page weight from the response, phone and social links from the page itself, recency from the newest date the page carries. Nothing here comes from a directory listing.

What the checks cannot tell us. A date in the markup proves the page was touched, not that the business is trading. Three entries carry an explicit warning about this. Two sites — Beaufort History Museum and the Aiken County Historical Museum — sit behind bot protection and could not be read at all, so they are absent rather than rejected.

Time zones. Both areas are on Eastern Time: five hours behind London, seven behind Kyiv. Their morning, from 8 to 11, is our afternoon. That is the widest usable overlap the United States offers us, and the reason this sheet is not about the West Coast.
