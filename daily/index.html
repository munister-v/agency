How this was checked. Every site was fetched on 1–2 September 2026 and read directly: platform from the markup, page weight from the response, phone and social links from the page itself, recency from the newest date the page carries. Nothing here comes from a directory listing.

What the checks cannot tell us. A date in the markup proves the page was touched, not that the business is trading. Three entries — 03, 11 and 19 — carry no 2025 or 2026 date at all and are flagged accordingly: confirm on social before spending a call. Facebook links cannot be verified by status code, because Facebook answers 200 even for a handle that does not exist; every Facebook address here was copied from a link the business publishes on its own site. Two sites, Beaufort History Museum and the Aiken County Historical Museum, sit behind bot protection and could not be read at all, so they are absent rather than rejected.

Time zones. Both areas are on Eastern Time: five hours behind London, seven behind Kyiv. Their morning, from 8 to 11, is our afternoon. That is the widest usable overlap the United States offers us, and the reason this sheet is not about the West Coast.
