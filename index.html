Munister Agency is a full-cycle IT studio: one team designs, builds, ships and keeps things running - no relay race between agencies and no lost context along the way. Since 2024 we've taken 10+ products from a blank page to real production: media platforms people actually read and listen to, ERP systems running real warehouses, fintech and edtech tools handling real users, and archival projects preserving real history.

Web & product, automation & AI, business systems - from a one-page site to an ERP with inventory and payments. Every project has its own domain and real users.

We work as a small team: design, code, launch and support in one pair of hands, no subcontractors. The stack - from static to Next.js, Flask and PostgreSQL - fits the task.

Architecture, deliberately chosen

One stack is not a strategy.

We choose the lightest architecture that can carry the product safely. A campaign site should not inherit SaaS complexity; an ERP should not be built like a landing page.

Fifty states,
one working day.

We are a European studio and we do not pretend otherwise. What matters to a U.S. client is simpler than a flag on a page: how many hours a day we are awake at the same time, and who answers when something breaks. Both are on this map.

Every state, by postal code

Overlap is calculated live from the IANA time-zone database against a 9-to-5 workday in your state, so it stays right through both countries’ daylight-saving changes. Split states are named honestly: Florida’s Panhandle, West River in South Dakota and El Paso keep their own clocks. Boundaries come from the U.S. Census Bureau.
