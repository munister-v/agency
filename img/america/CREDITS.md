# Photographs

All four images are from the **Carol M. Highsmith Archive**, Library of Congress,
Prints & Photographs Division. Highsmith dedicated the archive to the public
domain: the photographs are free to use without permission, fee or attribution.
We credit them on the page anyway.

| File | Original | LCCN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `plate-rushmore.webp` | Mount Rushmore, South Dakota | 2011632454 |
| `plate-brooklyn.webp` | Brooklyn Bridge, New York, New York | 2011632099 |
| `plate-goldengate.webp` | Golden Gate Bridge, San Francisco, California | 2013633353 |
| `band-wheat.webp` | Classic Kansas field of waving wheat | 2011632245 |

Retrieved through Wikimedia Commons, then cropped and mapped to the studio's
two-colour ramp (ink `#121636` → paper `#e9dfd1`) so they sit inside the palette
instead of arriving as stock colour. Source files are not kept in the repo; the
originals stay available at loc.gov by their LCCN.
