# Photograph

`band-wheat.webp` is from the **Carol M. Highsmith Archive**, Library of Congress,
Prints & Photographs Division. Highsmith dedicated the archive to the public
domain: the photograph is free to use without permission, fee or attribution.
We credit it on the page anyway.

| File | Original | LCCN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `band-wheat.webp` | Classic Kansas field of waving wheat | 2011632245 |

Retrieved through Wikimedia Commons, then cropped and mapped to the studio's
two-colour ramp (ink `#121636` → paper `#e9dfd1`) so it sits inside the palette
instead of arriving as stock colour. The source file is not kept in the repo;
the original stays available at loc.gov by its LCCN.

A triptych of Brooklyn Bridge, Mount Rushmore and the Golden Gate opened the
coverage section for one release and was removed. Same archive, same treatment,
LCCNs 2011632099, 2011632454 and 2013633353, should it ever come back.
